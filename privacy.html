Privacy policy

In effect from 17 November 2026.

This policy covers two things: this website, and short-clipper, the program that uploads clips to the Mindclip YouTube channel. In short: the website collects nothing about you, and the program only ever signs in to the channel owner's own Google account.

This website

The site has no accounts, forms, cookies, analytics or advertising. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may record technical details of visits, such as IP addresses, in its own server logs under GitHub's privacy statement. The page loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, so your browser also makes a request to Google when it opens the page.

Who uses short-clipper

Only the channel owner. short-clipper is a personal tool that runs on the owner's own computer. It is not distributed, and no member of the public is asked to sign in to it. If you are not the channel owner, it does not access any of your Google data.

What it accesses in the owner's Google account

When the owner signs in with Google, short-clipper asks for these permissions and uses them only as described:

  • Upload videos to YouTube: to post clips to the owner's channel, with their titles, descriptions and tags.
  • View the YouTube account: to read the owner's own channel and video figures, such as views, likes and subscriber count.
  • View YouTube Analytics reports: to read the owner's own watch statistics, such as how much of each clip was watched.

What is stored, and where

  • The sign-in token Google issues is kept in a file on the owner's computer. It is not copied to any server.
  • A record of which clips were uploaded, with their YouTube links, is kept on the same computer.
  • Channel statistics are read when the owner asks for them and are not kept afterwards.

Who it is shared with

Google user data is not sold, not shared with other people or companies, not used for advertising and not used to train AI models. Statistics are shown only to the channel owner; when the owner asks for them from a phone, the summary is delivered to the owner's private chat through Telegram.

short-clipper's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also uses YouTube API Services, so the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy apply to that use.

How AI is used

To choose clips, the transcript of a publicly available lecture is sent to an AI service (Anthropic's Claude), which marks the passages worth clipping. That transcript is the lecture's spoken words. No Google account data is sent to the AI service.

Switching it off and deleting data

The owner can remove short-clipper's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and can delete the token file and upload records from their computer. Removing access stops all uploads and all reading of statistics.

People who appear in clips

Clips are taken from lectures and talks that are already public. If you appear in a clip and want it removed, write to the address below with a link to the clip and it will be taken down.
